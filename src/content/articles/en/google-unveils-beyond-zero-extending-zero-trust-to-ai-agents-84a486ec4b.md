- Google introduced Beyond Zero, a security model for the AI era that extends zero trust to autonomous AI agents, moving access decisions from the application level to individual actions and resources.
- Beyond Zero is based on five principles: action-level authorization, combination of static and dynamic controls, enriched context, automated investigations, and challenges or containment measures.
- The authors state that BeyondCorp's assumptions of human accessors, human-speed actions, and application-boundary trust are no longer sufficient for the AI era.
- Beyond Zero deployments are internal-only, components are not fully built, and further papers are planned.
