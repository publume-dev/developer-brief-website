- Gemini 3.8 Flash is now available in GitHub Copilot, including VS Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, cloud agent, Copilot app, JetBrains, Xcode, and Eclipse.
- Available to Copilot Pro, Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise users, with rollout being gradual.
- Billed at introductory provider pricing under usage-based billing through December 31, 2026.
- Enterprise and Business administrators can manage access via model policy in Copilot settings; new models are enabled automatically unless disabled.

For developers, the model offers strong performance on terminal-based coding tasks, though no specific benchmark data or comparison details are provided. This may impact developers using Copilot CLI or relying on terminal interactions.
