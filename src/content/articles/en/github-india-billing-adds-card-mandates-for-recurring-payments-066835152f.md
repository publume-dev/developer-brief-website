- GitHub customers in India can now use eligible saved credit cards for automatic recurring payments on monthly and annual bills.
- The update uses a payment mandate, also known as an electronic mandate, to authorize future charges in line with Reserve Bank of India requirements.
- There are no changes to how customers save or remove payment methods, and the feature applies to paid GitHub products for customers in India where recurring card payments are supported.
