- JetBrains said its development-related AI spending grew roughly tenfold in about six months, prompting centralization.
- The company built Central CLI, a shared access and accounting layer designed to preserve tool choice while adding visibility and control over AI consumption.
- Central CLI routes AI-tool requests through JetBrains' existing AI platform, letting the AI-credit system apply to third-party tools and letting managers configure spending limits.
- More than 1,000 JetBrains developers adopted Central CLI within weeks of its rollout.
- Coverage is partial: terminal-based agents and personal subscriptions remain outside the managed path.
- JetBrains is still developing policies for fairly distributing AI budgets between different users and teams.
- The report does not state whether Central CLI will be released publicly, nor does it provide quantitative cost-savings or performance results from the centralized setup.
