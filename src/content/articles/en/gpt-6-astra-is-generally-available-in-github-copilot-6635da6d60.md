- GPT-6 Astra is generally available and selectable in the model picker across VS Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, Copilot coding agent, Copilot app, github.com, GitHub Mobile, JetBrains IDEs, Xcode, and Eclipse.
- The model is available to Copilot Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise users, with rollout being gradual.
- Billing is usage-based at provider list pricing.
- Copilot Enterprise and Business plan administrators can manage access through model policy in Copilot settings.
