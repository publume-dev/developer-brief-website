- Grab reduced the share of mechanical tickets handled by analysts from 44% in February to 30% in June.
- Grab's five-level autonomy model defines agent ownership levels for analytics workflows, with human oversight retained at all levels.
- From March to May, self-service analytics answered without human involvement increased from 53% to 67% for metric requests, 63% to 90% for data pulls, and 50% to 81% for SQL requests.
- Grab's Spartan system uses more than 50 skills and 120 analysis frameworks to route analytics requests, including those from Slack.
- Grab maintains over 5,000 certified tables and metrics, 4,000 context documents, and 2,000 golden records to support AI agent reliability.
