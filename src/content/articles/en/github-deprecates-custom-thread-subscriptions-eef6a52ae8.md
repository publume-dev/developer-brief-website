- GitHub's changelog announces the Customize option will be removed from thread-level notification settings; only Subscribed and Not subscribed will remain.
- Existing custom thread subscriptions will be converted to Subscribed, so users will receive notifications for all events in those threads.
- No immediate action is required for most users; those relying on custom subscriptions should review their notification settings as the rollout progresses.
- The changelog does not specify an exact rollout timeline or effective date.
