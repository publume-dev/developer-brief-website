- Starting October 1, 2026, checks, workflow runs, and statuses will follow the same retention setting as artifacts and logs, with a default of 90 days, ending the previous 400+ day retention.
- Retention caps still apply; public repositories have a maximum retention of 90 days for checks, workflow runs, and statuses.
- The change is not retroactive; adjusting the retention setting will not restore previously evicted data.
