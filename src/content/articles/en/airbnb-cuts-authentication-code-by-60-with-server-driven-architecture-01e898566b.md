- Moving authentication decisions from clients to a server-side policy engine reduced Airbnb's authentication-related code by 60%.
- The web client bundle shrunk by 100 KB due to the server-driven redesign.
- Successful authentications rose by 2.6% and duplicate account creation dropped by 27%.
- In the first three months after launch, Airbnb ran over 20 experiments, with non-client experiments going from idea to measured result in days rather than weeks.
