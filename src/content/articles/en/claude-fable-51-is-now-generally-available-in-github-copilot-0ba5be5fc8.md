- Claude Fable 5.1 is now generally available in the model picker across VS Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, JetBrains, Xcode, and Eclipse, with a gradual rollout.
- Copilot Enterprise and Business administrators must enable the Claude Fable 5.1 policy in Copilot settings; the policy is off by default.
- Claude Fable 5.1 requires data retention by default for Anthropic safety classifiers; retained data is not used for training. Other Claude models except Fable 5 and 5.1 remain zero data retention.
- Eligible enterprise customers can use Claude Fable 5 and 5.1 with zero data retention through end of calendar year under a time-bound exemption while Anthropic rolls out Enterprise Frontier Safeguards; afterwards EFS will be required.
- Claude Fable 5.1 is available to Copilot Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise users and billed at provider list pricing under usage-based billing.
