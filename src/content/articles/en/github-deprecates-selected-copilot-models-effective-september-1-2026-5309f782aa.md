- GitHub deprecated multiple AI models in Copilot: Gemini 3.1 Pro, Claude Opus 4.5 and 4.6, Claude Sonnet 4.5 and 4.6, and Raptor Mini.
- The deprecation is effective September 1, 2026.
- Suggested replacement models are listed in the announcement, though specific details are not included in the report.

The report does not specify whether the deprecation applies to all Copilot subscription tiers or only certain ones beyond individual annual plans.
