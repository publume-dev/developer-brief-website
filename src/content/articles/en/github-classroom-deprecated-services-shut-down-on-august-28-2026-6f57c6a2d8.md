- As of August 28, 2026, GitHub Classroom is deprecated, and its website, APIs, and related services are decommissioned.
- User accounts, repositories, and organizations created for Classroom are not affected by the deprecation.
- Some Classroom data (e.g., names, tests, LTI rosters) will be permanently deleted in accordance with GitHub's retention policies.
