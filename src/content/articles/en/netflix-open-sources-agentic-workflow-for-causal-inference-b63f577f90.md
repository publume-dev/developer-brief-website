- Netflix open-sourced an agentic workflow for Observational Causal Inference (OCI) that automates causal analysis tasks, including sensitivity analysis, using an actor-critic loop with human oversight.
- The workflow was evaluated on the ACIC competition dataset and found to be "competitive" against benchmark systems.
- In a case study on estimating the impact of new entertainment types on retention, the workflow produced an estimated effect that was "just 25% of the baseline" from a simple linear regression, with the critic agent flagging issues such as early adopter bias and a failed placebo test.
