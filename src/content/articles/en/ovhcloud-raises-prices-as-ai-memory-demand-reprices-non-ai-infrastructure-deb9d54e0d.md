- Starting in September, OVHcloud will raise prices for most of its catalog; 2026-edition gaming servers rise 87% and other recent servers 40-59%.
- Memory prices indexed to June 2025 reached 604 by June 2026, with OVHcloud paying 6x the June 2025 price for RAM, forecast to rise to 9x by September 2026 and 12x by early 2027.
- From October 1, OVHcloud separates storage and IP addresses as line items on Gen3 instances at €0.000146 per GB per hour and €0.0027 per hour.
- OVHcloud is dropping one-month, six-month, and 24-month saving plans, keeping 12- and 36-month options that lock pricing.
- Founder Octave Klaba attributes price increases to AI demand consuming fabrication capacity for high-bandwidth memory, affecting standard DDR4/DDR5, SSDs, and hard drives.
