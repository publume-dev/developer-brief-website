- Cloudflare OS is now open source: an AI application platform where each user's apps run in separate sandboxes; the capability-based Gatekeepers model restricts resource access, masks sensitive columns, applies rate limits, and requires human approval for destructive actions.
- The platform is released under Apache-2.0 via the official cloudflare-os repository and a starter deployment template on GitHub.
- In internal use since May 2026, non-technical staff built more than 4,000 custom business tools within 30 days, sales teams recovered an estimated 10,000 hours of manual data aggregation, and automated review agents flagged nearly 250,000 potential bugs, blocked 16,000 non-compliant merges, and caught roughly 600 architectural defects.
