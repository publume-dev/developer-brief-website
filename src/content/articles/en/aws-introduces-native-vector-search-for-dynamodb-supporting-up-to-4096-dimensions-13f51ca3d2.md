- DynamoDB now supports native vector search, enabling storage of embeddings and execution of approximate nearest-neighbor queries through the new SearchVectors API.
- Vector indexes support up to 4096 dimensions and offer Euclidean, cosine, and dot product distance functions, along with inline filtering.
- Pricing for vector indexes is based on data written, data processed during search, and data stored, all metered per byte and billed per GB, on top of standard DynamoDB charges.

The report mentions upcoming support through the ExtendDB adapter for local development and self-managed deployments, but details are not provided.
